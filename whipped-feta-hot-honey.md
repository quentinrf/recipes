# Whipped Feta with Hot Honey, Chili Oil & Toasted Almonds

*Serves 6–8 | Prep: 20 min | Cook: 10 min*

---

## Ingredients

### For the whipped feta
- 8 oz block feta (standard cow's-milk feta works great — see notes), drained
- 4 oz full-fat cream cheese, room temp
- 2–3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 small garlic clove (optional)

### For the hot honey
- 1/3 cup honey
- 1/2–1 tsp red pepper flakes
- Small pinch of salt
- Splash of apple cider vinegar

### For the quick chili oil (optional, instead of store-bought chili crisp)
- 1/4 cup neutral oil
- 1 tbsp red pepper flakes
- 1 small garlic clove, minced
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- Pinch of salt

### To serve
- 1 baguette or other sturdy rustic bread, sliced on a bias
- Olive oil, for brushing
- 1/4 cup almonds, roughly chopped and toasted (or pepitas)
- Flaky salt
- A few torn basil or mint leaves (optional)

---

## Instructions

### 1. Make the hot honey
Warm honey, red pepper flakes, and salt gently in a small pot over low heat for 3–4 minutes — don't let it boil hard. Off heat, stir in the vinegar. Cool and jar it; keeps for weeks at room temp.

### 2. Make the chili oil (if using)
Warm the oil in a small pot until shimmering, then pour over the red pepper flakes, garlic, sesame seeds, and salt in a heatproof bowl. It'll sizzle — let it cool, then jar it. Keeps for a couple of weeks in the fridge.

### 3. Whip the feta
Break the feta into chunks in a food processor. Add cream cheese, olive oil, lemon juice, and garlic if using. Blitz until completely smooth and fluffy, scraping down once or twice. Taste and adjust with a touch more lemon or oil rather than salt — feta varies a lot in saltiness. Pack into a jar or container.

### 4. Prep the bread
Brush baguette slices with olive oil and grill or toast (400°F oven, ~8 min, or a hot grill pan) until golden with a little char. Cool completely and store airtight — it recrisps fine sitting out.

### 5. Toast the almonds
Toast in a dry pan for 2–3 minutes until fragrant, then roughly chop.

---

## Tips & Notes

- **Feta choice:** Once whipped, the texture difference between sheep's-milk and cow's-milk feta mostly disappears, so standard block feta is the right call here — save the pricier sheep's-milk version for a dish where you're tasting it straight.
- **Skip the specialty chili crisp:** A jar of the trendy stuff runs $10–13 for one dish's worth of use. The five-minute homemade chili oil above gets you the same crunchy heat for pennies — or skip the chili element entirely and let the hot honey and flaky salt carry the heat/salt balance.
- **Nuts:** Toasted almonds or pepitas give the same crunchy, toasted payoff as pistachios for a fraction of the cost. If you want to splurge, pistachios are a nice color pop, but they're an upgrade, not a necessity.
- **Make ahead:** Whipped feta keeps refrigerated up to 3 days; hot honey and chili oil keep at room temp/fridge for up to 2 weeks; toasted bread and almonds hold airtight overnight.
- **Boat-day assembly:** Bring the feta jar in the cooler, everything else at room temp. Build at the table — spread feta on bread, drizzle hot honey, spoon on chili oil, scatter almonds and flaky salt.
