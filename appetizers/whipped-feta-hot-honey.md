# Whipped Feta with Hot Honey, Chili Oil & Toasted Almonds

*Whipped feta is one of the highest reward-to-effort dishes in the appetizer canon — five minutes in a food processor turns a block of feta into something closer to a mousse. Hot honey and chili oil do the rest of the work.*

**Serves:** 6–8
**Prep:** 20 min | Cook: 10 min | **Total:** 30 min

---

## Components

1. Hot honey — make this first, it needs to cool
2. Quick chili oil (optional) — make alongside the honey
3. Whipped feta
4. Toasted bread and almonds

---

## Ingredients

### Whipped Feta
- 8 oz (225g) block feta, drained (standard cow's-milk feta — see notes)
- 4 oz (115g) full-fat cream cheese, room temperature
- 2–3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 small garlic clove (optional)

### Hot Honey
- 1/3 cup (80ml) honey
- 1/2–1 tsp red pepper flakes
- Small pinch of salt
- Splash of apple cider vinegar

### Quick Chili Oil (optional, replaces store-bought chili crisp)
- 1/4 cup (60ml) neutral oil
- 1 tbsp red pepper flakes
- 1 small garlic clove, minced
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- Pinch of salt

### To Serve
- 1 baguette or other sturdy rustic bread, sliced on a bias
- Olive oil, for brushing
- 1/4 cup (30g) almonds, roughly chopped
- Flaky salt
- A few torn basil or mint leaves (optional)

---

## Method

### Make the hot honey
Warm the honey, red pepper flakes, and salt in a small pot over low heat for 3–4 minutes — just enough for the chili to bloom and the honey to loosen. Do not let it boil; scorched honey turns bitter fast. Off the heat, stir in the vinegar — this is what keeps the honey from tasting flat and one-note sweet. Cool and jar it. Keeps for weeks at room temperature, so make extra.

### Make the chili oil, if using
Warm the oil in a small pot until it shimmers and just starts to move, then pour it over the red pepper flakes, garlic, sesame seeds, and salt in a heatproof bowl — it should sizzle audibly on contact. Let it cool completely before jarring. Keeps a couple of weeks in the fridge.

### Whip the feta
Break the feta into rough chunks in a food processor. Add the cream cheese, olive oil, lemon juice, and garlic if using. Blitz continuously, scraping down the sides once or twice, until it's completely smooth and aerated — you're looking for the texture of whipped cream cheese, not a chunky spread. Taste and adjust with more lemon or oil rather than salt; feta's saltiness varies a lot batch to batch, and it's easy to overcorrect. Pack into a jar.

### Toast the bread and almonds
Brush baguette slices with olive oil and grill or toast — 400°F/200°C oven, about 8 minutes, or a hot grill pan — until golden with real char at the edges, not just pale tan. Let cool completely before storing; it recrisps fine sitting out for a few hours. Toast the almonds in a dry pan over medium heat for 2–3 minutes, tossing often, until fragrant and just golden, then roughly chop.

---

## Assembly / To Serve

Build this at the table, not in the kitchen — it's meant to be interactive. Spread a generous layer of whipped feta on a piece of toasted bread, drizzle with hot honey, spoon over a little chili oil, and finish with a scatter of toasted almonds, flaky salt, and torn herbs if using. Let people control their own heat level; the chili oil is potent enough that not everyone will want the same amount.

---

## Tips & Notes

- **Feta choice:** once whipped, the texture difference between sheep's-milk and cow's-milk feta mostly disappears — the processor does the work sheep's milk's natural creaminess would otherwise be doing. Save the pricier sheep's-milk feta for a dish where you're tasting it straight, unwhipped.
- **Skip the specialty chili crisp.** A jar of the trendy stuff runs $10–13 for maybe two tablespoons of actual use in this recipe. The five-minute chili oil above gets you the same crackling heat for pennies — or skip the chili element entirely and let the hot honey and flaky salt do the work.
- **Nuts:** toasted almonds give you the same crunchy, toasted payoff as pistachios at a fraction of the cost. If you want to splurge, pistachios add a nicer color, but they're an upgrade, not a necessity — don't let the recipe be gatekept by an expensive nut.
- **Make ahead:** whipped feta keeps refrigerated up to 3 days; hot honey and chili oil keep 2+ weeks; toasted bread and almonds hold airtight overnight.
- **Boat-day transport:** feta jar in the cooler, everything else rides at room temperature. Assemble at the table.
- **Wine:** a dry rosé or a citrusy, low-oak white — Assyrtiko or unoaked Chardonnay both work — stands up to the honey's sweetness without getting cloying.
